# Honey Chain AI Decision-Support Modules

## 1. Scope & Philosophy
Honey Chain uses AI strictly for **advisory decision support**. It does not make unsupported veterinary or chemical authenticity claims. Wording throughout the interface explicitly uses terms like:
- *Disease Risk Indicators*
- *Requires Field Inspection*
- *Estimated Honey Yield*

## 2. Implemented Modules

### A. Transparent Multi-Factor Hive Health Scoring
- Brood thermal stability (Optimal ~34.8°C): 35% weight
- Relative humidity balance (Optimal 50–65%): 25% weight
- Weight trend / nectar surplus: 25% weight
- Acoustic agitation frequency: 15% weight

### B. Brood Comb Disease Risk Screening
Combines:
- Visual comb cell perforation and uncapping patterns
- Thermal micro-fluctuation signatures
- Acoustic flight frequencies (>600 Hz indicating queen distress or swarming)
- Beekeeper qualitative notes

### C. Honey Yield Prediction
Forecasts harvest-ready comb honey (kg) based on weight accumulation trajectory over 7–14 days and regional multi-floral nectar flow seasons.
