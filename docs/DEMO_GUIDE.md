# Honey Chain Complete Hackathon Demo Storyline

Follow this 7-step sequence for a complete live hackathon jury presentation:

---

### Step 1: Beekeeper Sign In & Live IoT Monitoring
1. Click **Sign In** in the top navigation and select **Ramesh Patil (Beekeeper)** or enter `beekeeper@example.com` / `password123`.
2. Notice the authenticated session is established and shows "Ramesh Patil · Beekeeper".
3. Point out the **Multilingual Switcher** (switch to **हिंदी** or **मराठी** and back to English).
4. Review the **Live Hive Telemetry Card** (Box H023).
5. Click through the **IoT Simulation Sandbox** buttons (`Optimal Flow`, `Heat Stress Alert`, `Swarming Drop`) to show live dynamic gauge responses and health score recalculation.

---

### Step 2: Smart Alert → 1-Click Support Ticket (USP)
1. Go to **IoT & Alerts** tab.
2. View the **High Brood Temperature Warning (38.6°C)**.
3. Click **Raise Support Ticket**.
4. Show how the ticket **automatically pre-fills** the hive ID, alert ID, sensor readings, and health score!
5. Submit ticket to KVIC extension desk.

---

### Step 3: AI-Assisted Disease & Yield Decision Support
1. Navigate to **AI Disease Risk Scanner** under Dashboard.
2. Choose **Sample A: Suspected Varroa / Comb Perforations**.
3. Click **Run AI Risk Analysis** to display model confidence (88%), supporting factors, and approved organic thymol treatments.
4. Open **Honey Yield Predictor** to show the estimated harvestable honey (8.5 kg).

---

### Step 4: Harvest & Minting New Honey Batch
1. Go to **Honey Batches** tab.
2. Click **Mint New Honey Batch**.
3. Enter harvest details and click **Confirm & Tokenize Batch**.
4. Show simulated blockchain transaction receipt and persistent QR code binding.

---

### Step 5: Public Consumer Batch Verification (Zero Login)
1. Click **Sign Out** to experience the app as a public consumer. Notice that all private beekeeper controls disappear and the public verification portal is prominently displayed.
2. Under **Verify Your Honey**, enter the batch number printed on the bottle: `HC-MH-NAS-2026-00047`.
3. Click **Verify Batch**.
4. Show the **Existing Persistent QR Code** (it returns the exact same QR every time).
5. Click **View Traceability Journey** to show the complete 7-step blockchain audit trail from hive to bottle.
6. Test counterfeit detection: enter `HC-FAKE-9999` to show the **Counterfeit Warning** screen.

---

### Step 6: Beekeeper Registration & Marketplace
1. Click **Register Apiary** to demonstrate new beekeeper onboarding.
2. Enter new beekeeper details (Village, District, Years of experience).
3. Click **Register & Start Beekeeping**. Show how an authentic new Beekeeper ID (e.g. `BK-MH-NAS-XXXX`) and initial hive ledger are created instantly in persistent local storage.
4. Open **Marketplace** to show direct farm-to-consumer listings linked to verifiable batch tokens.
5. Open **Profit Calculator**: adjust hive numbers and honey yield sliders to calculate net income and margin per hive.

---

### Step 7: KVIC Admin Cluster Administration
1. Click **Sign In** and select **Dr. Anil Joshi (KVIC Admin)** or enter `admin@example.com` / `admin123`.
2. Open **Cluster Admin** to inspect the Nashik and Pune cluster aggregate metrics, active hives health distribution, regional production totals, and respond to beekeeper support tickets on the **Support Desk**.
